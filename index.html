Dueday support

Dueday is a subscription calendar for iPhone. It shows when each subscription charges, the last day to cancel before the next charge (the Cancel-by date), and step-by-step cancel guides with links to each service's own page.

Get help

Questions, bugs or ideas: open a support request. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

Does Dueday cancel subscriptions for me?
No. Dueday shows you the steps and opens the right page. You cancel on the service's website or in your App Store settings, then tap "Mark as cancelled".

Do I need an account or a bank login?
No. You add subscriptions yourself. Everything stays on your iPhone.

How do I cancel Dueday Premium?
Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Dueday Premium. Or open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

How do I restore a purchase?
In Dueday, open Settings and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore on the Premium screen.

Why didn't I get a reminder?
Reminders are part of Premium and need notifications to be allowed in iPhone Settings, Notifications, Dueday.
