Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Dueday is made so your information stays with you. This policy explains what the app does with data.

What stays on your device

The subscriptions you add (names, prices, currencies, billing dates, categories, trial dates, reminder settings, price changes) and your quiz answers are stored only on your iPhone. We do not have servers that receive them, and there is no account or sign-in.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, bank details or email inbox. Dueday does not link to bank accounts and does not track you across apps or websites. There are no ads.

Network requests the app makes

  • Exchange rates: to convert currencies, Dueday downloads public exchange rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). The request contains no personal data, but like any web request it reaches that service from your internet connection.
  • Purchases: Premium is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those purchases.
  • Links you open: cancel guides open the service's own website or your App Store subscriptions page. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by the app itself. Nothing is sent to us.

Deleting your data

Delete a subscription in the app, or delete Dueday from your iPhone to remove all of its data.

Children

Dueday is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
